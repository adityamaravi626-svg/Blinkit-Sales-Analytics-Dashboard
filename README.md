# Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## Project Overview

The **Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel.

The objective of this project is to analyze supermarket sales performance across different product categories and outlet types. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, charts, PivotTables, and interactive slicers.

It helps management understand overall sales performance, identify high-performing product categories, compare outlet performance, and analyze sales distribution and variation.

The dashboard is designed to present business information in a simple and understandable format, supporting better business decisions.

## Business Problem

The supermarket's sales data was available in raw format, making it difficult for management to:

- Understand overall sales performance.
- Identify the highest- and lowest-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across different outlet types.
- Analyze product visibility and its relationship with sales.
- Understand sales distribution and variation.
- Identify unusually high or low sales values.

This project addresses these challenges by creating an interactive Excel dashboard that presents key business metrics and statistical insights in one place.

## Project Objectives

The project focuses on four major objectives.

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

- Calculate total sales revenue.
- Analyze average sales value per record.
- Understand average product visibility.

### 2. Product Category and Sales Comparison

- Analyze sales contributions from the top four product categories.
- Identify the highest- and lowest-performing categories.
- Compare sales across different outlet types.

### 3. Outlet Performance and Sales Relationships

- Compare Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlets.
- Analyze the relationship between product visibility and sales.
- Compare category sales contributions across outlet types.

### 4. Statistical Analysis of Sales

- Calculate average, median, minimum, and maximum sales.
- Analyze sales variation using standard deviation and variance.
- Identify unusually high and low sales values.
- Compare average sales across categories, outlet types, and outlet locations.

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard includes KPI cards, category comparisons, outlet performance charts, product visibility analysis, statistical metrics, and interactive filters.
